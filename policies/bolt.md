## 1. Who Can Use Company Transport

### 1.1 Eligibility
All permanent employees of Bolt Technologies, of any grade, may use company transport if their home is at least 3 km from the office. There is no grade restriction: senior leaders are eligible on exactly the same terms as everyone else, and no travel allowance is paid in place of transport.

### 1.2 Contract and Probation Staff
Contract staff, consultants and people supplied by staffing agencies are not eligible for company transport. Permanent employees on probation are eligible from their first working day.

### 1.3 Proof of Address
Eligibility and routing are based on the address in the employee's latest address proof uploaded to the HR portal. Employees must upload a new proof within 15 days of moving home; until they do, trips continue to be routed to the old address.

## 2. Booking Window

### 2.1 When to Book
Trips must be booked at least 2 hours before the scheduled pickup or drop time. The booking window opens 7 days in advance, so a trip for next Monday can be booked from this Monday onwards.

### 2.2 Recurring Bookings
Employees with a fixed shift can set up a recurring booking for up to four weeks at a time. A recurring booking must be renewed before it ends; the app sends a reminder three days before it expires.

### 2.3 Changing a Booking
Changing the time or address of a trip is treated as cancelling the original trip and booking a new one. The change must therefore be made at least 2 hours before the new scheduled time, and also within the cancellation deadline of the original trip.

## 3. Cancellation

### 3.1 Cancellation Deadline
Trips may be cancelled in the app up to 1 hour before the scheduled time without any penalty.

### 3.2 Late Cancellations
A cancellation made less than 1 hour before the scheduled time is recorded as a no-show and counts towards the limits in Section 9.

### 3.3 Exceptions for Urgent Work
A late cancellation is not counted as a no-show if the employee's manager confirms in the app, within 24 hours, that the employee was held back for urgent work.

## 4. Night Drops and Women's Safety

### 4.1 Security Guard on Late Drops
For drops after 10:00 PM, a security guard accompanies the cab whenever a female employee is on board, and a female employee is never dropped last.

### 4.2 Evening Drops Without a Guard
Drops between 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM do not include a security guard. These trips are tracked live by GPS from the Bolt command centre, and any unscheduled stop longer than 5 minutes triggers an automatic call to the driver.

### 4.3 Safe Arrival Confirmation
Female employees on drops after 10:00 PM must tap "Reached Home" in the app once they are inside. If the button is not pressed within 15 minutes of the drop, the command centre calls the employee.

## 5. Weekend Transport

### 5.1 Saturday Service
Company transport runs on Saturdays only, on a reduced set of routes. Saturday trips must be booked by 5:00 PM on Friday; the normal 2-hour booking window does not apply to Saturdays.

### 5.2 Saturday Timings
Saturday pickups start no earlier than 7:00 AM, and the last Saturday drop leaves the office at 6:00 PM.

### 5.3 Sundays and Public Holidays
There is no company transport on Sundays or public holidays. Employees who choose to work on these days make their own travel arrangements, and the cost is not reimbursed.

## 6. Airport and Ad-hoc Trips

### 6.1 Approval
Airport and other ad-hoc trips require prior approval from the HR Head. Requests must be raised in the app at least 12 hours before the trip, with the business reason.

### 6.2 Cost Limit
A single ad-hoc trip is capped at INR 2,000. Airport parking and toll charges are included within the cap.

### 6.3 Unapproved Trips
A trip taken without the HR Head's prior approval is not paid for by the company, even if the HR Head approves it afterwards.

## 7. Cost Cap

### 7.1 Regular Trip Cap
Regular trips are capped at INR 1,200 per employee per trip.

### 7.2 Trips Above the Cap
If an employee's share of a trip exceeds the cap, Bolt pays the difference. Nothing is recovered from the employee. The transport team reviews such trips every quarter and adjusts routes to bring them under the cap.

## 8. Guest Travel

### 8.1 No Guests
Guests and visitors are not permitted to use company transport under any circumstances. This includes family members, interview candidates and employees of client companies.

### 8.2 Arranging Visitor Travel
Teams hosting visitors should book them a taxi through the corporate travel desk. The cost is charged to the hosting team's budget, not to the transport budget.

## 9. No-Show Penalty

### 9.1 Definition
A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board within 3 minutes of the cab arriving at the pickup point, or cancels less than 1 hour before the trip.

### 9.2 Penalties
Three no-shows in a calendar month result in a written warning sent to the employee and their manager. Five no-shows in a calendar month result in suspension from company transport for two weeks.

### 9.3 Resetting the Count
The no-show count resets on the first day of every calendar month. Written warnings stay on the employee's transport record for six months.
