## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Eligible Employees
Company transport is available to full-time employees in grades L1 to L6 whose home address is at least 5 km from the office. The distance is measured by road, along the shortest route calculated by the MoveInSync routing engine from the home address recorded in the HR system. The address an employee types into the app is used only to place the pickup pin and does not change eligibility. Employees who move house must first update their address in the HR system; eligibility is recalculated within three working days of the update.

### 1.2 Senior Grades
Employees in grade L7 and above are not eligible for company cabs. Instead, they receive a monthly travel allowance through payroll, as stated in their compensation letter. An L7 employee who is temporarily assigned to a night shift may request a one-time exception from the Transport Manager. An exception is granted for at most one month at a time and must be renewed in writing.

### 1.3 Interns and Trainees
Interns are eligible only for drops at the end of a night shift, meaning a shift that ends at or after 11:00 PM. Interns are not eligible for morning pickups, ad-hoc trips or airport trips. Graduate trainees in their first six months are treated as grade L1 employees for every purpose of this policy.

## 2. Booking and Cut-off

### 2.1 How to Book
All pickups and drops are booked through the MoveInSync app. Employees on a fixed roster are booked automatically every week from their shift schedule, and only need to act when their plans change. Bookings made by phone, email or chat with the transport desk are not accepted, except during an app outage announced by the transport team.

### 2.2 Booking Cut-off
A new booking, or a change to an existing booking, must be made at least 4 hours before the scheduled pickup or drop time. The app blocks changes after the cut-off. For example, an employee with a 7:30 PM drop must make any change by 3:30 PM the same day.

### 2.3 Late Requests
A request made after the cut-off is not guaranteed. The transport desk will try to place the employee on an existing route that has a free seat, but will not add a new cab or re-route other employees to accommodate a late request. If no seat is available, the employee must make their own travel arrangements, and that cost is not reimbursed.

## 3. Cancellation

### 3.1 Cancellation Window
A trip can be cancelled without penalty up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup or drop time, using the cancel option in the app. A cancellation made less than 2 hours before the trip is treated as a no-show and counts towards the limit in Section 9.

### 3.2 Leave and Work From Home
When leave or work-from-home is applied for in the HR system before 6:00 PM on the previous day, that day's trips are cancelled automatically. Leave approved after that time does not cancel trips automatically; the employee must cancel in the app within the cancellation window.

### 3.3 Cancellations by the Company
If Acme or the cab vendor cancels a trip, for example because of a road closure or a vehicle breakdown, the transport desk informs the employee at least 30 minutes in advance wherever possible. A company cancellation never counts as a no-show, and the employee may claim the cost of alternative travel up to the ad-hoc trip limit in Section 6.

## 4. Night-Shift Safety

### 4.1 Escort Requirement
For any drop scheduled after 9:00 PM, a trained security escort travels in the cab whenever a female employee is on board. The escort is provided by the cab vendor and must carry a photo identity card issued by the vendor. If the escort does not arrive, the cab must not leave the office until the transport desk arranges a replacement.

### 4.2 Routing Rules
On drops scheduled after 9:00 PM, a female employee is never the first person picked up or the last person dropped. If the planned route would leave her as the last drop, the routing engine changes the drop order; if that is not possible, the escort stays in the cab until she has been dropped.

### 4.3 Reaching Home Safely
On every night drop, the driver must wait until the employee confirms in the app that she has entered her residence. If no confirmation is received within 10 minutes, the command centre calls the employee and, if she cannot be reached, alerts the local security team.

## 5. Weekend and Holiday Transport

### 5.1 No Weekend Service
Company transport is not provided on Saturdays, Sundays or declared company holidays. This applies to all grades and all shifts. A drop at the end of a Friday shift that finishes after midnight is treated as a Friday trip and is provided as normal.

### 5.2 Working on a Weekend or Holiday
Employees asked by their manager to work on a weekend or holiday must arrange their own travel and claim the cost through the reimbursement portal within 30 days. The claim must include the manager's written approval and the trip receipt, and is paid up to the regular trip cap in Section 7.

### 5.3 Holiday List
HR publishes the list of company holidays for each location every January. Transport follows the holiday list of the office location, not of the employee's home state.

## 6. Ad-hoc and Airport Trips

### 6.1 What Counts as Ad-hoc
An ad-hoc trip is any trip outside the employee's regular roster, such as a client visit, an early start for a product release, an airport trip for official travel, or a hospital visit during working hours.

### 6.2 Approval
Every ad-hoc trip, including every airport trip, must be approved in advance by the employee's Transport Manager in the MoveInSync app. The request must state the business reason. A trip taken without prior approval is not paid for by the company.

### 6.3 Cost Limit
The cost of a single ad-hoc trip is capped at INR 1,500. Toll and parking charges are paid on top of the cap. Any amount above the cap is recovered from the employee's salary.

## 7. Cost Cap

### 7.1 Regular Trip Cap
The cost of a regular rostered trip is capped at INR 800 per employee per trip. The cap applies to the employee's share of a shared cab, not to the full cab fare.

### 7.2 Amounts Above the Cap
If an employee's share of a trip exceeds the cap, for example because they live far from the office or the cab carried fewer passengers than planned, the excess is recovered from the employee's salary at the end of the month. The employee is notified in the app before any deduction is made.

### 7.3 Disputing a Deduction
An employee who believes a deduction is wrong can raise a dispute in the app within 15 days of receiving the payslip. The transport team responds within 7 working days, and any amount wrongly deducted is refunded in the next payroll cycle.

## 8. Guest Travel

### 8.1 Who Can Travel as a Guest
Visitors, interview candidates and employees from other Acme offices may use company transport as guests.

### 8.2 How to Request
The host employee must raise a guest request in the app at least 24 hours before the trip, giving the guest's full name, mobile number and pickup address. Guest requests must also be approved by the host's Transport Manager.

### 8.3 Host Responsibility
The host employee is responsible for the guest for the whole trip. Guests are not allowed on any drop that requires a security escort.

## 9. No-Show Penalty

### 9.1 What Counts as a No-Show
An employee is marked as a no-show if they do not board the cab within 5 minutes of its arrival at the pickup point, or if they cancel less than 2 hours before the trip. The driver records the no-show in the app, and the employee receives an alert immediately.

### 9.2 Penalty
An employee who is a no-show twice in a calendar month is suspended from company transport for one week, starting the following Monday. During the suspension, the employee must make their own travel arrangements at their own cost.

### 9.3 Repeat Suspensions and Appeals
An employee suspended more than once in any three-month period is reported to their reporting manager. A no-show can be appealed in the app within 3 days if it was caused by the cab arriving at the wrong location or more than 15 minutes late.
